Semiconductors — Formula Master Sheet

Zero fluff. Pure equations, diode circuits, Zener regulators, and Boolean algebra tested in JEE Main.

FORMULA 01
Mass Action Law & Carrier Concentrations
$$n_e \cdot n_h = n_i^2$$
• Intrinsic semiconductor: $n_e = n_h = n_i$.
• n-type (Donors $N_D$): $n_e \approx N_D$, and minority hole density $n_h = \frac{n_i^2}{N_D}$.
• p-type (Acceptors $N_A$): $n_h \approx N_A$, and minority electron density $n_e = \frac{n_i^2}{N_A}$.
• Crucial Rule: Mass action law is valid at thermal equilibrium under constant temperature regardless of doping level.
FORMULA 02
Current & Electrical Conductivity ($\sigma$)
$$I = I_e + I_h = e A \left( n_e v_e + n_h v_h \right)$$ $$\sigma = e \left( n_e \mu_e + n_h \mu_h \right), \quad \rho = \frac{1}{\sigma}$$
• $e = 1.6 \times 10^{-19}\text{ C}$, $\mu_e$ is electron mobility, $\mu_h$ is hole mobility.
• Mobility relation: $\mu_e > \mu_h$ always because conduction electrons move in empty conduction band while holes move via bound valence states.
• Current density: $J = \sigma E = e (n_e \mu_e + n_h \mu_h) E$.
FORMULA 03
Dynamic (AC) & Static (DC) Diode Resistance
$$r_d = \frac{\Delta V}{\Delta I} \quad (\text{Dynamic/AC Resistance}), \qquad R_{dc} = \frac{V}{I} \quad (\text{Static/DC Resistance})$$
• Dynamic resistance is the inverse slope of the forward $I\text{-}V$ characteristic: $r_d = \left(\frac{dI}{dV}\right)^{-1}$.
• Typical forward dynamic resistance: $10\ \Omega \text{ to } 100\ \Omega$.
• In reverse bias below breakdown: resistance is virtually infinite ($r_d \sim 10^6\ \Omega$).
FORMULA 04
Rectifier Efficiencies & Ripple Factors
$$\text{Half-Wave: } V_{dc} = \frac{V_0}{\pi}, \quad \eta_{max} = \frac{0.406 R_L}{r_f + R_L} \approx 40.6\%, \quad f_{ripple} = f_{in}, \quad r = 1.21$$ $$\text{Full-Wave: } V_{dc} = \frac{2V_0}{\pi}, \quad \eta_{max} = \frac{0.812 R_L}{r_f + R_L} \approx 81.2\%, \quad f_{ripple} = 2f_{in}, \quad r = 0.482$$
• For 50 Hz input mains: Half-wave ripple frequency = 50 Hz, Full-wave ripple frequency = 100 Hz.
• Capacitor filter: $r \approx \frac{1}{4\sqrt{3} f C R_L}$ (Full-wave) — larger $C$ and larger $R_L$ yields flatter DC.
FORMULA 05
Zener Diode Voltage Regulator (6 Questions in JEE 2026!)
$$V_L = V_Z = \text{Constant}, \qquad I_L = \frac{V_Z}{R_L}$$ $$I_S = \frac{V_{in} - V_Z}{R_S}, \qquad I_Z = I_S - I_L = \frac{V_{in} - V_Z}{R_S} - \frac{V_Z}{R_L}$$
• Zener operates in REVERSE BREAKDOWN. If $V_{in} < V_Z$, Zener does not conduct ($I_Z = 0$), acting as open circuit.
• Maximum Zener current: $I_{Z,max} = \frac{P_{Z,max}}{V_Z}$.
• Minimum series resistance to prevent blowout: $R_{S,min} = \frac{V_{in,max} - V_Z}{I_{Z,max} + I_L}$.
FORMULA 06
Optoelectronic Devices & Cutoff Wavelength
$$\lambda_{cutoff} = \frac{hc}{E_g} \approx \frac{12400 \text{ \AA}}{E_g(\text{eV})} = \frac{1240 \text{ nm}}{E_g(\text{eV})}$$
• Photodiode: Operated in REVERSE BIAS because fractional change in minority current ($\frac{\Delta I}{I_0}$) is orders of magnitude easier to measure than majority current.
• LED: Operated in FORWARD BIAS using direct bandgap semiconductors ($E_g \ge 1.8\text{ eV}$ for visible light).
• Solar Cell: Unbiased ($V=0$); operates in the 4th quadrant of the $I\text{-}V$ graph.
FORMULA 07
Logic Gates Truth Equations (8 Questions in JEE 2026!)
$$\text{AND: } Y = A \cdot B \qquad \text{OR: } Y = A + B \qquad \text{NOT: } Y = \bar{A}$$ $$\text{NAND: } Y = \overline{A \cdot B} \qquad \text{NOR: } Y = \overline{A + B}$$ $$\text{XOR: } Y = A\bar{B} + \bar{A}B = A \oplus B \qquad \text{XNOR: } Y = AB + \bar{A}\bar{B} = \overline{A \oplus B}$$
• XOR Rule: Output is 1 only when inputs are DIFFERENT ($0 \oplus 1 = 1, 1 \oplus 0 = 1, 1 \oplus 1 = 0, 0 \oplus 0 = 0$).
• Universal Gates: Any Boolean expression can be synthesized using NAND alone or NOR alone.
FORMULA 08
De Morgan's Laws & Boolean Simplification
$$\overline{A + B} = \bar{A} \cdot \bar{B} \qquad \text{(Break the line, change the sign)}$$ $$\overline{A \cdot B} = \bar{A} + \bar{B}$$ $$A + \bar{A}B = A + B, \qquad A(A + B) = A, \qquad A + AB = A$$
• Double inversion identity: $\overline{\overline{A}} = A$.
• Redundancy theorem: $AB + \bar{A}C + BC = AB + \bar{A}C$ (The term $BC$ is redundant).
FORMULA 09
Universal Gate Realization Minimum Gate Counts
$$\begin{array}{|c|c|c|} \hline \textbf{Target Gate} & \textbf{NANDs Required} & \textbf{NORs Required} \\ \hline \text{NOT} & 1 & 1 \\ \text{AND} & 2 & 3 \\ \text{OR} & 3 & 2 \\ \text{XOR} & 4 & 5 \\ \text{XNOR} & 5 & 4 \\ \hline \end{array}$$
• Memorize the symmetric count sequence: NAND pattern is 1, 2, 3, 4, 5 for NOT, AND, OR, XOR, XNOR.
• NOR pattern mirrors it: 1, 3, 2, 5, 4!
FORMULA 10
Temperature Coefficient & Band Gap Energy
$$n_i(T) = A \cdot T^{3/2} e^{-\frac{E_g}{2 k_B T}}$$ $$\alpha = \frac{1}{\rho}\frac{d\rho}{dT} < 0 \quad (\text{Negative Temperature Coefficient})$$
• At $T = 0\text{ K}$, semiconductor behaves as a perfect insulator (valence band completely full, conduction band completely empty).
• As $T \uparrow$, covalent bonds rupture exponentially $\implies n_e, n_h \uparrow \implies \text{Conductivity } \sigma \uparrow, \text{Resistivity } \rho \downarrow$.
• Silicon bandgap: $E_g \approx 1.1\text{ eV}$. Germanium bandgap: $E_g \approx 0.7\text{ eV}$.
