Gravitation — Formula Master Sheet

Zero fluff. Pure equations, dimensional scaling, and exact limits tested in JEE Main.

FORMULA 01
Newton's Universal Law of Gravitation
$$F = G \frac{m_1 m_2}{r^2}, \quad \vec{F}_{12} = -G \frac{m_1 m_2}{r^2} \hat{r}_{12}$$
• $G = 6.674 \times 10^{-11}\text{ N}\cdot\text{m}^2/\text{kg}^2$ [Dimensions: $[M^{-1} L^3 T^{-2}]$].
• Valid strictly for point masses or spherically symmetric mass distributions.
FORMULA 02
Acceleration Due to Gravity ($g$) at Surface
$$g = \frac{GM}{R^2} = \frac{4}{3} \pi G \rho R$$
• At constant density $\rho$: $g \propto R$.
• At constant mass $M$: $g \propto 1/R^2$. If Earth shrinks by $x\%$ with mass fixed, $g$ increases by $2x\%$.
FORMULA 03
Variation of $g$ with Height ($h$)
$$\text{Exact: } g_h = g \left(\frac{R}{R+h}\right)^2 = \frac{g}{\left(1 + \frac{h}{R}\right)^2}$$ $$\text{Approx } (h \ll R): g_h \approx g \left(1 - \frac{2h}{R}\right), \quad \frac{\Delta g}{g} \approx -\frac{2h}{R}$$
• JEE Trap Alert: Use the approximation $g(1 - 2h/R)$ ONLY when $h \le 0.05R$ ($\approx 320\text{ km}$). For $h = R$ or $h = 2R$, you MUST use the exact square law!
FORMULA 04
Variation of $g$ with Depth ($d$)
$$g_d = g \left(1 - \frac{d}{R}\right), \quad \frac{\Delta g}{g} = -\frac{d}{R}$$
• Strictly Exact Everywhere: Holds from surface all the way to center ($d = R \implies g_{\text{center}} = 0$).
• Crucial JEE Ratio: $\Delta g_h = 2 \Delta g_d$ for the same numerical distance ($h = d \ll R$).
FORMULA 05
Variation with Latitude ($\lambda$) Due to Earth's Rotation
$$g' = g - \omega^2 R \cos^2 \lambda$$
• At Equator ($\lambda = 0^\circ$): $g_{\text{eq}} = g - \omega^2 R$ (Minimum gravity).
• At Poles ($\lambda = 90^\circ$): $g_{\text{pole}} = g$ (Maximum gravity, zero centrifugal effect).
• Weightlessness at equator requires: $\omega = \sqrt{g/R} \approx 17\times$ current Earth spin!
FORMULA 06
Gravitational Potential ($V$) for a Uniform Solid Sphere
$$\text{Outside } (r \ge R): V_{\text{out}} = -\frac{GM}{r}$$ $$\text{Surface } (r = R): V_s = -\frac{GM}{R}$$ $$\text{Inside } (r \le R): V_{\text{in}} = -\frac{GM}{2R^3} (3R^2 - r^2)$$ $$\text{Center } (r = 0): V_{\text{center}} = -\frac{3}{2} \frac{GM}{R} = 1.5 V_s$$
• Potential at center is NEVER zero! It is the deepest gravitational well ($1.5\times$ surface value).
FORMULA 07
Gravitational Potential Energy ($U$) & Elevation Work
$$U(r) = -\frac{GMm}{r}, \quad \Delta U = U_h - U_{\text{surface}} = \frac{mgh}{1 + \frac{h}{R}}$$
• For $h \ll R$: $\Delta U \approx mgh$.
• For $h = R$: $\Delta U = \frac{1}{2} mgR$.
• For $h = \infty$: $\Delta U = mgR$ (Escape energy required from rest).
FORMULA 08
Orbital Velocity ($v_o$) & Kepler's 3rd Law
$$v_o = \sqrt{\frac{GM}{r}} = \sqrt{\frac{GM}{R+h}}, \quad T = \frac{2\pi r}{v_o} = 2\pi \sqrt{\frac{r^3}{GM}}$$ $$T^2 = \left(\frac{4\pi^2}{GM}\right) r^3 \implies T^2 \propto r^3$$
• Near surface orbit ($h \approx 0$): $v_o = \sqrt{gR} \approx 7.92\text{ km/s}$, $T \approx 84.6\text{ minutes}$.
• Speed depends ONLY on central mass $M$ and orbit radius $r$, completely independent of satellite mass $m$!
FORMULA 09
Satellite Energy Triad & Binding Energy
$$K = \frac{GMm}{2r}, \quad U = -\frac{GMm}{r}, \quad E = K + U = -\frac{GMm}{2r}$$ $$E = -K = \frac{U}{2}, \quad \text{Binding Energy } B = -E = \frac{GMm}{2r}$$
• To eject a satellite from orbit to infinity, supply energy $+K$.
• To double the orbit radius ($r \to 2r$), energy needed is $\Delta E = \frac{GMm}{4r}$.
FORMULA 10
Escape Velocity ($v_e$)
$$v_e = \sqrt{\frac{2GM}{R}} = \sqrt{2gR} = \sqrt{\frac{8}{3} \pi G \rho R^2} = R \sqrt{\frac{8}{3} \pi G \rho}$$ $$v_e = \sqrt{2} \cdot v_o \approx 11.2\text{ km/s (for Earth)}$$
• Escape speed is strictly INDEPENDENT of projection angle ($\theta$) and projectile mass ($m$).
• If projected from height $h$: $v_e = \sqrt{\frac{2GM}{R+h}}$.
