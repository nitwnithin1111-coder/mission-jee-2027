Story 1: The Invisible Cosmic Glue (Universal Law of Gravitation)
"Why doesn't the Moon crash into Earth? Why does an apple drop?" — Newton's Insight

Every mass in the universe attracts every other mass. The force acts along the line joining their centers. It gets stronger if masses are bigger, and drops sharply if they move apart: F = G × M₁ × M₂ / r².

g = GM / R² Surface gravity (point mass / solid sphere)
g = (4/3) · π · G · ρ · R In terms of planet density ρ: g ∝ ρ · R
  • Analogy: If two planets are the exact same size, but Planet A is made of solid gold (dense) and Planet B is made of wood (light), Planet A pulls you far harder (g ∝ ρ).
  • Mass vs Radius Trap: If mass doubles (2M) with radius constant → gravity doubles (2g). But if radius doubles (2R) with mass constant → gravity drops to one-fourth (g/4) because of the power of 2!
Story 2: Flying High (How Gravity Drops as You Climb into Space)
"Imagine Earth's gravity as a Wi-Fi signal radiating from the core"

Gravity does not care where the grass is. It only measures distance from the dead center of Earth (r = R + h). Because of the inverse-square law, climbing up weakens gravity faster and faster.

gh = g · [ R / (R + h) ]² = g · [ R / r ]² EXACT FORMULA: Use when h is large (h = R/2, R, 2R)
gh ≈ g · (1 - 2h / R) BINOMIAL APPROX: ONLY for small heights (h < 5% of R, < 320 km)
Fractional Drop: Δg / g = 2h / R % Drop = (2h / R) × 100% (e.g. h = 1% R → g drops 2%)
⚡ The 5-Second Mental Rule ("Flip & Square"):
1. Find total distance from center: r = R + h. Express it as a fraction of R.
2. Flip the fraction, square it, and multiply by surface weight or g!
• At ground (h = 0): Distance 1R → (1/1)² = 1 → g (Full weight)
• At h = R / 2: Distance 1.5R = (3/2)R → flip is 2/3 → (2/3)² = 4/9 → (4/9) g (Weight = 72 × 4/9 = 32 N)
• At h = R: Distance 2R → flip is 1/2 → (1/2)² = 1/4 → g / 4 (Weight drops to 25%)
• At h = 2R: Distance 3R → flip is 1/3 → (1/3)² = 1/9 → g / 9 (Weight drops to 11.1%)
Story 3: Journey to the Center of Earth (Variation with Depth d)
"Newton's Onion Shell Theorem: Why you get LIGHTER as you dig down!"

Most students guess: "Distance to center decreases, so gravity must increase!" Wrong! As you dig down to depth d, the entire sphere of rock above your head pulls you upward, canceling out! Only the smaller inner ball of Earth below your feet pulls you downward.

gd = g · (1 - d / R) EXACT AT ANY DEPTH: Pure linear straight line! No power of 2.
% Drop at depth d = (d / R) · 100% d = R/2 → 50% drop. d = R → 100% drop (g = 0).
  • At Earth's Center (d = R): gcenter = g(1 − 1) = 0. Earth pulls you equally in all directions. You float weightless!
  • Equal Drop Rule (Height vs Depth): For small h, drop at height is 2h/R, while drop at depth is d/R. They are equal when: d = 2h (or h = d / 2). Going 5 km up loses as much gravity as digging 10 km down!

Story 4: Cosmic Speed Limits (Orbital Velocity vs. Escape Velocity)
"Newton's Mountain Cannon: Fire a cannonball faster and faster..."

Imagine standing on a super-tall mountain and firing a cannonball horizontally:
1. Low Speed: Curves and hits the ground.
2. Exact Orbital Speed (vo): The ground curves away under the cannonball at the exact same rate it falls! It enters a circular orbit, falling forever without hitting.
3. Escape Speed (ve): Fired so fast that Earth's gravity can never pull it back to a halt. It breaks the gravitational cage permanently.

vo (Orbital Velocity) = √(GM / r) = √(gR) Near surface: vo ≈ 7.92 km/s (≈ 8 km/s)
ve (Escape Velocity) = √(2GM / R) = √(2gR) From Earth surface: ve ≈ 11.2 km/s
The Golden Link: ve = √2 · vo ≈ 1.414 · vo Escape speed is exactly 41.4% faster than orbital speed!
  • Proper Name for vo: Orbital Velocity (vo) — the minimum horizontal velocity needed to put a satellite into orbit around Earth at distance r.
  • Proper Name for ve: Escape Velocity (ve) — the minimum speed given to a body from the surface so that it escapes Earth's gravitational field completely to infinity.
  • Mass Independence: Both vo and ve do NOT depend on the mass of the projectile (a 1-gram pebble and a 100-ton rocket need the exact same 11.2 km/s to escape Earth).
  • Angle Independence: Escape speed ve does NOT depend on launch angle θ (straight up, 45°, or sideways—any path that avoids hitting the ground escapes).
Story 5: Kepler's Cosmic Clock (Planetary Laws)

Johannes Kepler analyzed decades of planetary orbits and discovered 3 laws that govern every satellite and planet:

1. Law of Orbits: Ellipses All planets orbit in ellipses with the Sun at one focus (not center!).
2. Law of Areas: dA/dt = L / (2m) = Constant Equal areas in equal times. Result of Angular Momentum Conservation!
3. Law of Periods: T² ∝ r³ ⇒ (T₁ / T₂)² = (r₁ / r₂)³ Farther satellites travel slower and take much longer years.
  • Speed at Perihelion vs Aphelion: Because angular momentum L = m v r is conserved: when a planet is closest to the Sun (Perihelion, small r), it flies fastest (vmax). When farthest (Aphelion, large r), it moves slowest (vmin). v1r1 = v2r2.
Story 6: Satellite Energies & Gravitational Potential
"Gravity is attractive, so Potential Energy is ALWAYS negative!"

At infinity (infinitely far away), potential energy is zero. Since gravity pulls things inward, you must do negative work to bring a mass in from infinity.

Gravitational Potential: V(r) = - GM / r Scalar. Measured in J/kg. Negative everywhere!
Kinetic Energy (K) = + GMm / (2r) Always positive motion energy: K = ½ m vo²
Potential Energy (U) = - GMm / r U = 2 × Total Energy = - 2K
Total Mechanical Energy (E) = - GMm / (2r) E = K + U = - K. Negative means BOUND orbit!
Binding Energy = + GMm / (2r) = |E| Energy needed to free the satellite from Earth to infinity.
⚡ The 1-2-1 Energy Ratio Trick: For any circular satellite orbit of radius r:
|Kinetic Energy| : |Potential Energy| : |Total Energy| = 1 : 2 : 1
• If Kinetic Energy is +100 J → Potential Energy is −200 J → Total Energy is −100 J → Binding Energy is +100 J!

OFFICIAL ANSWER KEY & DERIVATIONS

Verified Solutions (Source: NTA Official Keys)

Question 1 Solution Correct Answer: (A) 32 N
  1. Since h = R / 2 is large (not tiny), use exact formula: gh = g · [ R / (R + h) ]².
  2. Total distance from center: r = R + R / 2 = (3/2)R.
  3. gh = g · [ R / ((3/2)R) ]² = g · (2/3)² = (4/9) g.
  4. Weight: Wh = (4/9) × Wsurface = (4/9) × 72 = 32 N.
⚠️ Trap: Do NOT divide by 2! 72 / 2 = 36 N is the NTA distractor. Gravity follows an inverse-square law.
Question 2 Solution Correct Answer: (B) 5 / 9
  1. At height h = R / 2: g1 = g · [ R / (R + R/2) ]² = (4/9) g.
  2. At depth d: gd = g · (1 − d / R).
  3. Equate both: g(1 − d / R) = (4/9)g → 1 − d / R = 4/9 → d / R = 1 − 4/9 = 5 / 9.
Question 3 Solution Correct Answer: (C) 22.4 km/s
  1. Escape speed formula: ve = √(2GM / R).
  2. For new planet: M' = 2M and R' = R / 2.
  3. v'e = √[ 2G(2M) / (R/2) ] = √[ 4 × (2GM / R) ] = 2 × ve = 2 × 11.2 = 22.4 km/s.
Question 4 Solution Correct Answer: (B) d = 2h
  1. Small height (h ≪ R): gh = g (1 − 2h / R). Depth: gd = g (1 − d / R).
  2. Equate gh = gd → 2h / R = d / R → d = 2h (or h = d / 2).
Question 5 Solution Correct Answer: (B) 2 : 1
  1. Orbital speed formula: vo = √( GM / r ) → vo ∝ 1 / √r.
  2. Ratio: vA / vB = √( rB / rA ) = √( 4R / R ) = √4 = 2 : 1.
Question 6 Solution Correct Answer: (A) - 9 GM / r
  1. Field is zero at distance x from mass m: G m / x² = G(4m) / (r − x)².
  2. Square root both sides: 1 / x = 2 / (r − x) → r − x = 2x → x = r / 3. Distance from 4m is 2r / 3.
  3. Potential at this point: V = − G m / (r / 3) − G(4m) / (2r / 3) = − 3Gm / r − 6Gm / r = - 9 GM / r.
Question 7 Solution Correct Answer: (A) Increases by 2%
  1. Surface gravity formula: g = G M / R².
  2. For small percentage change (mass constant): Δg / g ≈ − 2 (ΔR / R).
  3. Given radius shrinks by 1% (ΔR / R = − 1%): Δg / g = − 2(− 1%) = + 2% (Increases by 2%).
