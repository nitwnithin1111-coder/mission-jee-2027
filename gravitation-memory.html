Card 01 • Surface Gravity Tap to flip
What is $g$ on the surface in terms of mass $M$ and uniform density $\rho$?
$$g = \frac{GM}{R^2} = \frac{4}{3} \pi G \rho R$$
👆 Tap to Reveal Formula
• Scaling: If density is constant, $g \propto R$. If mass is constant, $g \propto 1/R^2$.
Card 02 • Exact Variation with Height Tap to flip
What is the exact formula for $g_h$ at any arbitrary altitude $h$ above Earth?
$$g_h = \frac{GM}{(R+h)^2} = g \left( \frac{R}{R+h} \right)^2 = \frac{g}{\left( 1 + \frac{h}{R} \right)^2}$$
👆 Tap to Reveal Formula
• Never use binomial approximation when $h$ is large like $h = R$ or $h = 2R$!
Card 03 • Small Altitude Approximation Tap to flip
What is the fractional and percentage decrease in $g$ when $h \ll R$?
$$g_h \approx g \left( 1 - \frac{2h}{R} \right), \qquad \frac{\Delta g}{g} = -\frac{2h}{R}$$
👆 Tap to Reveal Formula
• Valid only for $h < 320\text{ km}$ ($5\%$ of Earth's radius).
Card 04 • Variation with Depth Tap to flip
What is $g_d$ at depth $d$ below the surface? What is $g$ at the Earth's center?
$$g_d = g \left( 1 - \frac{d}{R} \right) = \frac{g}{R} r$$ $$\text{At center } (d=R, r=0): \quad g_{center} = 0$$
👆 Tap to Reveal Formula
• $g$ varies strictly LINEARLY with distance from center inside a uniform solid sphere.
Card 05 • Equal Drop in g Tap to flip
For small $h \ll R$, what depth $d$ produces the exact same decrease in $g$ as height $h$?
$$d = 2h$$
👆 Tap to Reveal Formula
• A $1\%$ drop at height $h = 32\text{ km}$ requires digging to depth $d = 64\text{ km}$.
Card 06 • Work Done to Elevate Mass Tap to flip
What is the minimum work required to lift mass $m$ to height $h$ above Earth?
$$W = \Delta U = \frac{mgh}{1 + \frac{h}{R}}$$ $$\text{For } h = R: \quad W = \frac{mgR}{1 + 1} = \frac{1}{2}mgR$$
👆 Tap to Reveal Formula
• Only when $h \ll R$ does $W \approx mgh$. Over cosmic distances, always divide by $(1+h/R)$!
Card 07 • Satellite Orbital Speed & Period Tap to flip
What is the orbital speed $v_o$ and time period $T$ of a satellite in orbit radius $r$?
$$v_o = \sqrt{\frac{GM}{r}} = \sqrt{\frac{GM}{R+h}}$$ $$T = \frac{2\pi r^{3/2}}{\sqrt{GM}} \implies T^2 = \left( \frac{4\pi^2}{GM} \right) r^3$$
👆 Tap to Reveal Formula
• Kepler's 3rd Law: $T^2 \propto r^3$. Near-earth satellite period is $T \approx 84.6\text{ minutes}$.
Card 08 • Satellite Energy Relations Tap to flip
State the exact relationship between Kinetic ($K$), Potential ($U$), and Total Energy ($E$).
$$K = \frac{GMm}{2r}, \qquad U = -\frac{GMm}{r}, \qquad E = -\frac{GMm}{2r}$$ $$E = -K = \frac{U}{2}, \qquad \text{Binding Energy} = -E = \frac{GMm}{2r}$$
👆 Tap to Reveal Formula
• Total energy is NEGATIVE for any bound elliptical or circular orbit.
Card 09 • Escape Velocity from Surface Tap to flip
What is the surface escape speed $v_e$, its relation to $v_o$, and its angle dependence?
$$v_e = \sqrt{\frac{2GM}{R}} = \sqrt{2gR} = \sqrt{2} \cdot v_o \approx 11.2\text{ km/s}$$
👆 Tap to Reveal Formula
• $v_e$ is strictly INDEPENDENT of projection angle ($\theta$) and mass of the object!
Card 10 • Variation with Earth's Rotation Tap to flip
What is the apparent gravity $g'$ at latitude $\lambda$ due to Earth's rotation ($\omega$)?
$$g' = g - R\omega^2 \cos^2\lambda$$ $$\text{Equator } (\lambda = 0^\circ): g_{eq} = g - R\omega^2 \qquad \text{Poles } (\lambda = 90^\circ): g_{pole} = g$$
👆 Tap to Reveal Formula
• $g$ is maximum at the poles and minimum at the equator. Weightlessness at equator occurs if $\omega = \sqrt{g/R}$ (17 times faster rotation).
