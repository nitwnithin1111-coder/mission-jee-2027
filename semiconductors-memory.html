Card 01 • Mass Action Law Tap to flip
What relates electron and hole density in doped semiconductors at thermal equilibrium?
$$n_e \cdot n_h = n_i^2$$ $$\text{In n-type: } n_e \approx N_D, \quad n_h = \frac{n_i^2}{N_D}$$
👆 Tap to Reveal Formula
• Valid at any doping concentration provided temperature remains constant.
Card 02 • Electrical Conductivity Tap to flip
What is the formula for conductivity $\sigma$ and total current $I$ in terms of mobilities?
$$\sigma = e \left( n_e \mu_e + n_h \mu_h \right)$$ $$I = e A \left( n_e v_e + n_h v_h \right) = e A E \left( n_e \mu_e + n_h \mu_h \right)$$
👆 Tap to Reveal Formula
• Electron mobility $\mu_e$ is always greater than hole mobility $\mu_h$ ($\mu_e > \mu_h$).
Card 03 • Diode Dynamic Resistance Tap to flip
How is dynamic (AC) diode resistance defined from an $I\text{-}V$ operating curve?
$$r_d = \frac{\Delta V}{\Delta I} = \left( \frac{dI}{dV} \right)^{-1}$$
👆 Tap to Reveal Formula
• Under forward bias, typical $r_d$ is $10\text{ to }100\ \Omega$. Under reverse bias, $r_d \sim 10^6\ \Omega$ (infinite ideal).
Card 04 • Full-Wave Rectifier Ripple Tap to flip
What is the DC output voltage and output ripple frequency for a Full-Wave Rectifier?
$$V_{dc} = \frac{2V_0}{\pi}, \qquad f_{ripple} = 2 f_{in} = 100\text{ Hz (for 50 Hz input)}$$ $$\eta_{max} \approx 81.2\%$$
👆 Tap to Reveal Formula
• For Half-Wave: $V_{dc} = V_0/\pi$, $f_{ripple} = f_{in} = 50\text{ Hz}$, $\eta_{max} \approx 40.6\%$.
Card 05 • Zener Regulator Current Relations Tap to flip
State the node current equation (KCL) for source, Zener, and load in a regulator circuit.
$$I_S = I_Z + I_L \implies I_Z = \frac{V_{in} - V_Z}{R_S} - \frac{V_Z}{R_L}$$
👆 Tap to Reveal Formula
• Condition for regulation: $I_Z \ge I_{Z,min} > 0$. If $V_{in} < V_Z$, Zener turns OFF ($I_Z = 0$).
Card 06 • Maximum Zener Current Tap to flip
How do you find the maximum allowable current $I_{Z,max}$ given rated power $P_{max}$?
$$I_{Z,max} = \frac{P_{Z,max}}{V_Z}$$ $$R_{S,min} = \frac{V_{in,max} - V_Z}{I_{Z,max} + I_L}$$
👆 Tap to Reveal Formula
• If series resistance $R_S < R_{S,min}$, the Zener diode overheats and destroys itself.
Card 07 • Optoelectronic Cutoff Wavelength Tap to flip
What is the cutoff wavelength $\lambda_{max}$ to excite electrons across bandgap $E_g$?
$$\lambda_{cutoff} = \frac{hc}{E_g} \approx \frac{12400 \text{ \AA}}{E_g(\text{eV})} = \frac{1240 \text{ nm}}{E_g(\text{eV})}$$
👆 Tap to Reveal Formula
• Photodiode responds only to photons with $h\nu \ge E_g$ ($\lambda \le \lambda_{cutoff}$).
Card 08 • Photodiode vs LED Biasing Tap to flip
In which bias mode do Photodiodes, LEDs, and Solar Cells operate?
$$\text{Photodiode: } \textbf{Reverse Bias} \quad \left(\frac{\Delta I_{minority}}{I_{minority}} \text{ is huge}\right)$$ $$\text{LED: } \textbf{Forward Bias} \quad (\text{Direct bandgap, visible } E_g \ge 1.8\text{ eV})$$ $$\text{Solar Cell: } \textbf{Unbiased (4th Quadrant)}$$
👆 Tap to Reveal Formula
• Tested 3 times in JEE Main 2026!
Card 09 • De Morgan's Theorems Tap to flip
State both of De Morgan's Laws for Boolean logic gate algebra.
$$\overline{A + B} = \bar{A} \cdot \bar{B} \qquad (\text{NOR to AND inversion})$$ $$\overline{A \cdot B} = \bar{A} + \bar{B} \qquad (\text{NAND to OR inversion})$$
👆 Tap to Reveal Formula
• "Break the bar, change the sign".
Card 10 • Universal NAND Gate Realization Counts Tap to flip
How many NAND gates are needed to construct NOT, AND, OR, XOR, and XNOR?
$$\text{NOT: } 1, \quad \text{AND: } 2, \quad \text{OR: } 3, \quad \text{XOR: } 4, \quad \text{XNOR: } 5$$
👆 Tap to Reveal Formula
• Memorize the clean count: 1, 2, 3, 4, 5! For NOR gates, it mirrors to: 1, 3, 2, 5, 4.
