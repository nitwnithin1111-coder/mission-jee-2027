Student Trust & Safety

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 22, 2026 • Compliant with DPDP Act 2023 & Minor Protection Guidelines

Our Core Privacy Promise to Students & Parents: Mission JEE 2027 is an educational resource dedicated to helping students clear competitive engineering examinations. We do NOT sell student contact numbers, do NOT spam WhatsApp with coaching sales calls, and do NOT engage in behavioral advertisement tracking of minors.

1. Information We Collect

We believe in minimal data footprint. Most of Mission JEE 2027 can be used completely anonymously without creating an account:

  • Study Progress & Bookmarks (Stored Locally): Your chapter completion marks, bookmarked PYQ archetypes, and formula filters are saved directly in your browser's localStorage on your own device. They never touch our servers.
  • Voluntary Support & Grievance Submissions: When you submit a complaint, report a typo in a solution, or request a new chapter via our Support Portal, you may choose to provide your name, contact email or phone number, and issue details so our academic team can respond to your ticket.
  • Anonymous Usage Telemetry: Standard, privacy-preserving HTTP request logs (IP address, browser user-agent, pages accessed) to ensure server security, prevent DDoS attacks, and measure which chapters need more study materials.

2. Protection of Minors (High School Students)

Because our users are predominantly high school students aged 14 to 18 preparing for TS IPE and JEE Main examinations:

  • We strictly prohibit the commercial exploitation of student contact lists.
  • We never share, rent, or lease student information to private coaching centers, marketing agencies, or credit loan providers.
  • Parents and legal guardians possess the absolute right to request immediate deletion of any submitted feedback or contact information.

3. Downloadable Study Guides & PDF Offline Storage

All downloadable study guides (such as semiconductors-story-guide.pdf and gravitation-story-guide.pdf) are rendered dynamically and downloaded directly to your local file system. We do not track what you do with downloaded PDFs or how many times you review them offline.

4. Cookies & Local Storage

We do not use third-party tracking cookies or advertising pixels. We use standard browser localStorage strictly for necessary functional purposes (such as saving your active chapter filters, your study progress, and your submitted support ticket history).

5. Data Retention & Your Rights

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act 2023 and applicable privacy laws, you possess the right to:

  • Access & Review: View all tickets or feedback submitted under your email.
  • Correction: Update or rectify any reported information.
  • Erasure: Request the total deletion of your submitted contact records at any time.

6. Grievance Redressal & Contact Officer

In accordance with Indian Information Technology laws and DPDP regulations, for any privacy concerns, complaints, or data deletion requests, you may reach our designated Student Grievance Officer directly:

  • Designation: Student Grievance & Academic Support Officer
  • Email: grievances@missionjee2027.com
  • Online Portal: Raise a Request or Complaint Online
  • Headquarters: Hyderabad, Telangana, India
